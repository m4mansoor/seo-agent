Why link building is still the slow part

The problem

Links still decide rankings, and getting one has not changed in fifteen years: find a site that gives links, create an account, find the field or the editor, place the link, and check that it actually went live. Then do it again. Fifty links is fifty of those.

What actually goes wrong

  • You pay an agency $150 to $400 a link and never see the method or the site list. A new hire starts from a blank page.
  • You buy a list of "1,000 sites" and it is full of ghosts. Angelfire still scores DA 93 on a decade of old links, and it shut down years ago. Nobody fetched the URL.
  • The link goes up as plain text, or the host adds nofollow after you post. It looks fine on the page. Nobody checked.
  • Fifty links land in one afternoon, all reading the same phrase. That is the clearest signal links were bought, and you paid for it.

What the agent does instead

It knows the exact method for 1,138 sites, re-verified every month. It tells you the pace and anchor mix your site can carry, and why. It builds each link in a real browser, checks the live page for a real hyperlink with the right anchor, and logs it with proof. When a site asks for something it should not do alone, it stops and asks you.

How a link gets built

Everything it does

Tells you what you need before you start
Ask what it takes and it answers as a number: which sites are open to you right now, how many more open with a mailbox, which ones need you at the screen.
Plans the campaign with you, and says why
It reads how many sites already link to you and proposes a pace, an anchor mix, a followed share and how long to spread it over, each with a plain reason. Ask for more than is wise and it says so once, then builds what you asked. It is your site.
Knows the method for every site
Not "go to the site and sign up". The playbook for that kind of link, the steps with the exact button and field names, what the site requires, and corrections learned from earlier builds where the guide was wrong.
Builds the link in a real browser
Login-free sites build in seconds with a proof screenshot. Sites that need an account are built by the assistant in its own browser, signing up once and signing in ever after.
Verifies before it counts
It opens the public page and checks three things: is your URL a real hyperlink, does the anchor match, is it followed. Plain-text URLs and links the host quietly made nofollow do not count.
Stops at gates instead of faking its way through
A captcha, an email code or a social login is a gate. It tells you what the site wants in plain words and offers the ways through: clear it yourself, connect a service once, paste a code, or skip.
Reads your page for keywords and anchors
It suggests keywords from the target page itself, then spreads anchors across the five types so your profile is not one phrase forty times.
Reports what it built, from its own record
Every link with its anchor, where it lives, the site's authority and when it was placed. Not a backlink index that will not show a new link for weeks.
Watches the links afterwards
Weekly re-checks of everything it placed, every referring domain to your site, what is new and lost each month, spam flags and a disavow file.
Finds what competitors have and you do not
Point it at two or three competitors and it lists the referring domains they have that you lack, with the method to build each one attached.
Paces everything
Links arrive steadily across days and methods rather than in a burst. At most 5 a day and 20 a week to any one site, and you can run many sites under one subscription.
Never forgets what a site taught it
When a build finds the guide was wrong, which field gives the followed link, a length cap, a password rule, it records the fact. The next build on that site starts from the truth.

Where it beats a person, and where it does not

Better than a link builder at

  • Remembering. It knows the method for 1,138 sites on day one. A new hire knows the ones they have used before.
  • Checking every time. Link 47 at six in the evening gets the same verification as link 1. Plain text and nofollow never slip through because someone was tired.
  • Keeping the record. Every link, anchor, live URL and proof in one log, re-checked weekly. Not a spreadsheet nobody updates.
  • Pacing. It will not place fifty links in an afternoon because the deadline is Friday.
  • Cost. About $8 a month against $2,500 to $4,000 a month for a person, and it works at 2am in the chat you already use.

Still yours

  • The judgement. Which keywords matter, how fast to go, whether a site suits your brand. It advises with reasons and then does what you say.
  • The gates. It will not solve a captcha by pretending to be a person, log into your Google account unattended, or work around a site's rules. Links built that way are the ones that get removed.
  • The content. On sites that want an article, it writes to your brief. It will not spin nonsense to get past a moderator.
  • The honesty. A link it could not verify is reported as unverified, not counted. It never promises a ranking.
