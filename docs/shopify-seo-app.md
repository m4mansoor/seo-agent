# The Shopify app

**Status: in development.** The engine it runs on is built and tested. This page describes what the app will do. It is not in the App Store yet; star [the repository](https://github.com/m4mansoor/seo-agent) to hear when it is.

## What it is

An embedded Shopify app that builds real backlinks to the products and collections you actually want to rank, and proves each one went live. Built on Polaris and App Bridge, billed through Shopify, so there is no separate account and no key to copy.

## Targets, from your catalogue

Products, collections, pages, blog posts and the home page, listed with sales rank and inventory so you link to what sells rather than what happens to be first alphabetically. A whole collection can be one campaign: every product in it, three links each.

## Anchors from your own product data

Product titles, types, vendors and tags feed the keyword suggestions, and the app builds an anchor plan across exact, partial, branded, naked and generic so your link profile does not read as one phrase repeated forty times.

## Campaigns

The same plan preview as every other door: the exact sites, their Domain Authority, method and anchor, before anything runs. Filter by DA, dofollow share, method and tier. Watch each link move through queued, building, placed, unverified, gated or failed, with the proof screenshot inline. Templates for a new collection, a product launch, a seasonal sale page and a brand authority push.

## Gates

A captcha, an email code or a login appears as a banner with four ways through: paste the code, clear it yourself, connect a service once, or skip the site. Nothing is faked and no site's rules are worked around.

## Your own AI model

Account-based sites are driven by an AI key you supply, entered once in settings, with the estimated cost per link shown before a campaign runs.

## Watching and reporting

Weekly re-checks of every placed link. Every referring domain to your store, what is new and lost each month, spam flags, and a disavow file. Competitor gap against up to three competitor stores, with a method attached to every row that is in the library. Links per product against orders in the same period, if you grant the orders scope.

## Autopilot

When a product, collection or article is created, queue a template campaign for it after a delay you set, inside a velocity cap, so links arrive at a steady pace rather than in a burst.

## Billing and privacy

Charged through Shopify's own billing, which is required for App Store apps, so it appears on your Shopify invoice. The app reads products, collections, pages, blogs and the shop, and writes nothing to your store except its own settings. The mandatory data-request, customer-redact and shop-redact webhooks are implemented, and uninstalling deletes your store's data from the engine.

Meanwhile, the [command line and MCP version](https://github.com/m4mansoor/seo-agent) works today and can target any URL, including a Shopify store.
