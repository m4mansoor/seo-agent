## What high DA means for a backlink

Domain Authority predicts how well a domain ranks. A link from a DA 90 site passes more authority than one from a DA 30 site, but only if the page is indexed and the link is dofollow. This table marks both, so you are not chasing nofollow profile pages on famous domains.

Most of these sites need an account. SEO Agent's method for each one tells your assistant exactly which field takes the link, what the site requires, and how to verify the result.
